How the total is calculated

Simple interest: amount × yearly rate ÷ 365 × days overdue, plus any flat fee once the invoice is past due.

Can I charge any rate I want?

Not necessarily. Many places cap late fees or interest, and a charge normally needs to be in your contract or invoice terms before the work starts. Check your local rules or ask a lawyer. This tool only does the arithmetic.
