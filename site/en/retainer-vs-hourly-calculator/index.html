Reading the comparison

Below break-even hours the retainer pays more than hourly billing. Above it, hourly wins. The two scenario rows show how sensitive the deal is to the client's real usage.

When is a retainer the better deal?

When work is steady and predictable, and you'd rather have reliable income and an easier invoicing routine. It also stops the client from watching the clock on every message.

How do I protect myself on a retainer?

State the hours or deliverables it covers, what happens to extra work, and whether unused hours roll over. Without limits, a retainer can quietly turn into a discount.
