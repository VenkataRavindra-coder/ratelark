What an invoice should include

A unique invoice number, your details and the client's, the issue and due dates, itemised charges, the total owed, and how to pay you. Clear terms up front make it easier to chase a late payment.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser only. Nothing you type is sent to a server. Your details are remembered on this device so the next invoice starts filled in.
