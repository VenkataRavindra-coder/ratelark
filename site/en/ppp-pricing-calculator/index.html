Assumptions and overrides

Data: World Bank International Comparison Program price levels (CC BY 4.0); exchange rates from Exchange Rate API. Exchange rates move, so check the current rate before you publish prices.

How these prices are worked out

Each country's price level compares what the same basket of goods costs there versus the US. Your price is reduced by the share of that gap you choose to pass on, never below your minimum, then rounded to a friendly number in the local currency.

What is PPP pricing?

Purchasing power parity (PPP) pricing sets a lower price where money buys more, so people in lower-income countries can afford your product and you sell to more customers.

Is it safe to offer discounts by country?

Use regional prices if your payment platform supports them. Discount codes can be shared, so use them for a limited launch and watch for abuse. Never go below your costs.
